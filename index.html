About Me

My name is Ephraim Favour Mwaba, and I am a second-year Computer Science student at Mulungushi University in the School of Engineering and Technology. I have always been curious about how the websites and apps I use every day are actually built, and that curiosity is what led me to choose this programme. Outside of my coursework, I enjoy solving small programming problems, exploring new tools, and finding practical ways to apply what I learn in class to real projects. This website is part of my ICT251 Web Technologies coursework, and building it has helped me understand how HTML, CSS, and good structure come together to create a page that is both usable and easy to maintain. I am looking forward to growing my skills further as the semester continues.
